Car Maintenance Myths That Often Lead to Emergency Towing in Brisbane

Car maintenance advice is everywhere — from friends and family to social media and outdated mechanics’ tips. Unfortunately, believing the wrong advice can leave you stranded on the side of the road needing a 24-hour tow truck or urgent emergency towing Brisbane services.

At OG Towing, we regularly respond to breakdowns that could have been prevented with proper knowledge and timely servicing. In this blog, we uncover common car maintenance myths that often result in unexpected breakdowns and the need for a 24-hour towing service in Brisbane.

Myth 1: “If the Car is Running Fine, It Doesn’t Need Servicing”

Many drivers assume that if their vehicle starts and drives normally, everything is fine. However, internal wear and hidden issues can develop long before visible symptoms appear.

Skipping routine servicing can lead to:

  • Engine overheating
  • Worn brake components
  • Battery failure
  • Oil sludge buildup

These issues often escalate suddenly, leading to roadside breakdowns and the need for an emergency tow truck.

Fact: Modern engines operate under high pressure and temperature and engine oil begins to degrade after extended use, reducing lubrication efficiency and increasing internal wear. Regular oil changes significantly reduce the risk of engine damage.

Preventive maintenance is far cheaper than calling for emergency towing in the middle of the night.

Myth 2: “I Don’t Need to Check My Battery Until It Dies”

Car batteries rarely fail without warning. Slow cranking, dim headlights and electrical glitches are early signs many drivers ignore.

In Brisbane’s warm climate, batteries can degrade faster due to heat exposure. When ignored, a simple battery replacement turns into a call for 24-hour towing.

If you ever find yourself stuck with a dead battery and no way to jump-start your vehicle, professional roadside assistance Brisbane services can help — but prevention is always better.

Fact: Battery-related issues are one of the most common causes of roadside breakdowns in Australia, particularly during extreme heat conditions. Motoring data published by the Australian Automobile Association highlights that battery failure consistently ranks among the top reasons drivers require roadside assistance across the country.

Myth 3: “Overheating is Normal in Summer”

Some drivers believe minor overheating during hot Queensland days is normal. It’s not.

Ignoring temperature spikes can result in:

  • Blown head gaskets
  • Cracked radiators
  • Complete engine failure

Overheating is one of the most common reasons drivers require an urgent tow truck Brisbane service.

If your temperature gauge rises unexpectedly, it’s safer to stop driving and arrange emergency towing Brisbane rather than risk severe engine damage.

Myth 4: “Tyre Pressure isn’t That Important”

Underinflated or overinflated tyres are dangerous and increase the risk of:

  • Blowouts
  • Poor fuel efficiency
  • Loss of control
  • Uneven tyre wear

A sudden tyre blowout on a busy road often leads to an urgent need for a 24-hour tow truck — especially late at night.

Regular tyre pressure checks can prevent costly breakdowns and roadside emergencies.

Myth 5: “Strange Noises Can Wait”

Grinding brakes. Knocking sounds. Squealing belts.

Drivers often postpone inspections because the vehicle still “drives fine.” Unfortunately, small mechanical noises can quickly turn into major failures.

Brake failure and transmission damage are two common causes of late-night emergency towing requests handled by OG Towing.

If something sounds unusual, get it inspected immediately instead of risking a call to a 24-hour towing service.

Myth 6: “Short Drives Don’t Affect the Engine”

Frequent short trips without allowing the engine to reach optimal temperature can:

  • Increase carbon buildup
  • Reduce battery charge
  • Cause moisture accumulation in oil

Over time, this leads to breakdowns that may require assistance from a Capalaba towing service or surrounding Brisbane providers. Vehicles need occasional longer drives to maintain optimal engine health.
