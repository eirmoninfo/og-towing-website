What To Do If Your Car Breaks Down on the Gateway Motorway

If your vehicle breaks down, consider these steps:

  1. Move your vehicle to a safe location if possible.
  2. Turn on your hazard lights.
  3. Stay clear of moving traffic.
  4. Contact roadside assistance or a professional tow truck service.
  5. Provide your exact location and direction of travel.
  6. Explain the vehicle problem and destination.
  7. Wait in a safe location until assistance arrives.
