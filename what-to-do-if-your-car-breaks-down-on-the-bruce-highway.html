What to Do If Your Car Breaks Down on the Bruce Highway: A Complete Emergency Guide

Breaking down on the Bruce Highway can happen without warning, whether you're commuting, travelling for work, or heading on a Queensland road trip. A flat tyre, engine failure, battery issue, or collision can leave you stranded in a potentially dangerous situation, especially on one of Australia's busiest highways. Knowing exactly what to do can protect you, your passengers, and your vehicle while ensuring help arrives quickly.

In this complete emergency guide, you'll learn the essential steps to take after a vehicle breakdown, how to stay safe beside fast-moving traffic, when to call a professional tow truck, and why thousands of Queensland drivers rely on OG Towing for fast, affordable, and reliable Bruce Highway towing services.

Common Reasons Cars Break Down on the Bruce Highway

  1. Flat tyres
  2. Dead batteries
  3. Engine overheating
  4. Mechanical failures
  5. Fuel shortages
  6. Electrical faults
  7. Road accidents
  8. Transmission issues

Step 1 – Move to a Safe Location

  1. Pull onto the emergency shoulder.
  2. Avoid stopping around bends.
  3. Stay visible.
  4. Switch on hazard lights.
  5. Apply the handbrake.

Step 2 – Keep Yourself Safe

  1. Wear a reflective vest if available.
  2. Exit the vehicle only when safe.
  3. Stand behind the safety barrier.
  4. Keep children and pets away from traffic.
  5. Never stand in front of or behind the vehicle.

Step 3 – Assess the Situation

  1. Can the problem be fixed safely?
  2. Is it a flat tyre?
  3. Has the engine overheated?
  4. Is there accident damage?
  5. Is towing required?
